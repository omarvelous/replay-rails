# Analysis: Branding for RePlay

## The current name

**RePlay** — a play on "Real Estate Play." The content "plays" on
screens. You "replay" listings in storefront windows. It's short,
memorable, has a natural logo treatment (Re**Play** with the accent on Play).

### Strengths
- **Double meaning** — "Real Estate" + "Play" (playback/display)
- **Short** — 6 characters, one word, easy to say
- **Verb energy** — it's an action. "RePlay your listings."
- **Logo opportunity** — the play button ▶ icon is universal and ties to screens/video
- **Memorable** — people know the word "replay" from sports, music, video

### Weaknesses
- **Domain availability** — `replay.com` is almost certainly taken (premium domain, common English word). So are `replay.io`, `replay.app` (replay.io is a debugging tool, replayos.app exists)
- **Trademark crowding** — "Replay" is used by a fashion brand (Replay jeans), a video debugging tool (replay.io), and many others. Real estate is a different class, but brand confusion is real.
- **Generic feel** — "Replay" doesn't immediately signal real estate or signage. Could be a music app, a sports app, a video tool.
- **SEO challenge** — searching "replay" returns the fashion brand, the song, the debugging tool. Hard to rank.

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| **Memorability** | High | Office managers tell each other about it word-of-mouth |
| **Domain availability** | High | Must be acquirable at reasonable cost |
| **Trademark clearance** | High | Can't conflict with existing marks in real estate or tech |
| **Category signal** | Medium | Should hint at real estate or signage (but not required) |
| **Pronunciation** | Medium | Must be unambiguous when spoken |
| **Global readability** | Medium | Works across English-speaking markets |
| **Logo potential** | Medium | Should have a natural visual mark |
| **Length** | Medium | Under 10 characters ideal, under 15 acceptable |
| **SEO potential** | Low | Brand search is more important than keyword SEO |

## Top picks for evaluation

If keeping **RePlay**, the best domain paths are:
1. `replay.re` — short, clever, real estate nod
2. `getreplay.com` — proven startup pattern
3. `replayhq.com` — professional, available patterns

If renaming, the strongest candidates:

### 1. Showfront
- **Why:** "Show" (display content) + "front" (storefront). Exactly what
  the product does. Natural compound word. Easy to say.
- **Domain:** `showfront.com` — check availability
- **Logo:** A storefront window icon with content inside
- **Tagline:** "Your storefront, always showing"

### 2. Curbcast
- **Why:** "Curb" (curb appeal, street-level) + "cast" (broadcast).
  Signals outdoor, street-facing, content delivery.
- **Domain:** `curbcast.com` — likely available (unique compound)
- **Logo:** A signal/broadcast icon
- **Tagline:** "Cast your listings to the curb"

### 3. ReScreen
- **Why:** Keeps the "Re" prefix (real estate). "Screen" is literal.
  Clean, professional, descriptive.
- **Domain:** `rescreen.com` — check availability
- **Logo:** Screen/monitor with a play button
- **Tagline:** "Real estate, on screen"

### 4. RePane
- **Why:** "Re" (real estate) + "Pane" (window pane). The product lives
  in the window. Unique word, no conflicts.
- **Domain:** `repane.com` — very likely available
- **Logo:** A window pane divided into content zones
- **Tagline:** "Every pane tells a story"

### 5. Loopd
- **Why:** Content loops on the screen. Short (5 chars). Modern
  dropped-vowel pattern. Active verb.
- **Domain:** `loopd.com` — check availability
- **Logo:** A loop/infinity symbol
- **Tagline:** "Keep your listings in the loop"

## Branding elements beyond the name

### Visual identity

| Element | Current | Recommendation |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| **Primary color** | Indigo (#2f6bff / #5b8eff) | Keep — professional, tech-forward, not aggressive |
| **Logo mark** | Play button ▶ in gradient square | Works well. The play button is universal for "content plays here" |
| **Typography** | System fonts (Inter via -apple-system) | Consider a brand font for marketing (Inter is great, make it explicit) |
| **Tone** | "Your window, working 24/7" | Confident, practical. Right for office managers. |
| **Color palette** | Indigo + gray + white | Add a warm accent (amber or teal) for energy. Pure indigo is cold. |

### Brand voice

The audience is **non-technical office managers** at real estate brokerages.
They don't care about technology — they care about:
- "Does my window look professional?"
- "Is it easy to update?"
- "Will it bring people through the door?"

**Voice principles:**
- **Practical, not techy** — "Update your window in 2 clicks" not "cloud-based content management system"
- **Confident, not salesy** — "Here's how it works" not "revolutionary game-changing disruption"
- **Warm, not corporate** — "Your listings, looking their best" not "maximize property exposure throughput"

### Tagline candidates

| Tagline | Style |
|---------|-------|
| "Your window, working 24/7" | Current — practical, clear |
| "Every window is a lead" | Outcome-focused |
| "Your storefront, always selling" | Action-oriented |

| "The window that works after hours" | Time-specific value prop |
| "From window to phone in one scan" | QR-focused differentiator |

### Brand applications

| Touchpoint | Notes |
|------------|-------|
| **Marketing site** | Hero, features, pricing — needs the brand story front and center |
| **App UI** | Logo in sidebar, accent colors throughout |

| **Sales materials** | One-pager, pitch deck, case studies |
| **Social media** | Profile images, cover photos, post templates |

## Next steps

1. **Domain search** — check availability for top picks (`replay.re`, `showfront.com`, `curbcast.com`, `rescreen.com`, `repane.com`, `loopd.com`)
2. **Trademark search** — USPTO TESS search for top picks in relevant classes (Class 9: software, Class 35: advertising, Class 36: real estate)
3. **Gut check** — say each name out loud 10 times. "Hey, have you tried ____?" Which feels natural?
4. **Logo exploration** — sketch 3-4 mark concepts for the shortlisted name
5. **Domain acquisition** — secure the .com and relevant TLDs

The name decision should happen before the marketing site build (Phase 1
of the marketing plan) since it affects every URL, every page, every email.

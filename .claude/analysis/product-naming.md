# Analysis: Product Naming & Brand Vocabulary

## Context

RePlay needs a consistent vocabulary for marketing, sales, docs, and
in-product copy. The naming system should:

- Feel cohesive — everything sounds like it belongs together
- Be self-explanatory — an agent who's never seen the product should
  get the gist from the name alone
- Scale — new features slot into the naming pattern naturally
- Play on "RePlay" — the brand name is a verb, which gives us a
  built-in action vocabulary

---

## Naming Pattern

The strongest pattern is **RePlay + Noun** for major product lines,
and **plain descriptive names** for features within those lines.
Forced branding on every small feature creates confusion — save the
"RePlay ___" format for things that appear on the marketing site,
in sales decks, and on invoices.

---

## Product Lines (Top-Level — Marketing & Sales)

These are the things you'd list on a pricing page, mention in a
pitch, or put on a sales one-pager.

### The Platform

| Candidate | Feeling | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **RePlay** | Clean, simple | The platform is just "RePlay" — no suffix needed |
| RePlay Platform | Corporate | Only if you need to distinguish from the hardware |
| RePlay for Real Estate | Descriptive | Useful in cold outreach, not as a product name |

**Recommendation: RePlay.** The platform is the brand. No suffix.

### Passive Storefront Signage

The service where ads loop on a screen in a brokerage window.

| Candidate | Feeling | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **RePlay Display** | Clear, professional | "Display" = the screen showing content. Clean noun. |
| RePlay Window | Literal | Too narrow — also used in lobbies, not just windows |
| RePlay Signage | Industry term | Accurate but generic — sounds like a commodity |
| RePlay Loop | Playful | Nods to the looping playlist. Fun but maybe too casual |
| RePlay Showcase | Premium | "Showcase your listings" — works in copy, heavy as a name |
| RePlay Screen | Hardware-adjacent | Confuses the service with the physical device |

**Recommendation: RePlay Display.** It's what you said instinctively,
and it works. "Display" is both the noun (the screen) and the verb
(to show). "Your listings, on display" writes itself.

### Interactive Open House Kiosk

The portable touchscreen experience at open houses.

| Candidate | Feeling | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **RePlay Experience** | Premium, broad | Matches the model name. "Experience" implies interaction |
| RePlay Kiosk | Descriptive | Accurate but sounds like a mall directory |
| RePlay Touch | Modern | Emphasizes the interactive aspect. Short. |
| RePlay Open House | Use-case specific | Limits the product to one scenario (what about showings, events?) |
| RePlay Interactive | Tech-forward | Feels like a feature description, not a product name |
| RePlay Live | Energetic | "Live" = in-person + real-time. Could confuse with livestreaming |
| RePlay Present | Action-oriented | "Present your listing" — nice verb/noun duality |
| RePlay Engage | Marketing-speak | Too buzzwordy |

**Recommendation: RePlay Experience.** It's the right level of
premium for selling to agents. "Turn every open house into a RePlay
Experience" is a strong line. Also leaves room for non-open-house
use cases (private showings, broker events, lobby installations).

**Runner-up: RePlay Touch.** Shorter, more modern. Could work as a
sub-brand if "Experience" feels too abstract. "RePlay Touch — the
interactive listing display."

## Hardware (What You Sell or Rent)

### The HDMI Stick / Compute Device

The thing that plugs into a TV and runs the player app.

| Candidate | Feeling | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **RePlay Player** | Clean, obvious | It plays content. Matches the model name. |
| RePlay Stick | Form-factor specific | Only works for HDMI sticks, not if you ship a box |
| RePlay Hub | Tech-y | Implies it does more than playback |
| RePlay Device | Generic | Works but boring |

**Recommendation: RePlay Player.** Universally understood. Applies
whether it's a Fire TV Stick, a Pi, or a compute module inside a
larger display.

### The Portable Touchscreen TV

The ApoloSign / OEM display on wheels for open houses.

| Candidate | Feeling | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **RePlay Kiosk** | Industry-standard | Clearly communicates "freestanding interactive screen" |
| RePlay Display (portable) | Consistent | Same name as the service, qualified with "portable" |
| RePlay Screen | Simple | Conflicts with the Screen model (logical, not physical) |
| RePlay Board | Creative | "Digital board" — less techy than kiosk |
| RePlay Station | Premium | Feels like a permanent installation |
| RePlay Stand | Literal | Describes the form factor. Modest. |
| RePlay Canvas | Artistic | "Your listings on a canvas" — nice for real estate |
| RePlay Panel | Commercial | Sounds like a commercial signage product |

**Recommendation: RePlay Kiosk.** Agents will immediately understand
what it is. "We'll bring a RePlay Kiosk to your next open house"
paints a clear picture. It also differentiates from the Player (which
is just a compute stick) — the Kiosk is the full package: screen +
stand + wheels + player built in.

### Tablets (if you go there)

| Candidate | Feeling | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **RePlay Tablet** | Obvious | Works if you offer branded tablets |
| RePlay Mini | Size-relative | Implies a smaller version of the Kiosk |
| RePlay Go | Mobile | "Take RePlay on the go" — nice energy |
| RePlay Pocket | Too small | Tablets aren't pocket-sized |

**Recommendation: RePlay Tablet** for now, **RePlay Go** if you want
to brand the mobile/portable product line more aggressively. "RePlay
Go" could be an umbrella for both the Kiosk and Tablet — anything
portable.

## Features & Flows (In-Product Naming)

These are the terms used inside the product UI, in help docs, and in
onboarding. They don't need the "RePlay" prefix — they're features,
not product lines.

### Content Creation

| Thing | Current Name | Candidates | Recommendation |
|-------|-------------|------------|----------------|
| An individual ad unit | Ad | **Ad**, Slide, Card, Spot | **Ad** — universal, understood |
| A listing-focused ad | Listing Ad | **Listing Ad**, Property Spot, Listing Card | **Listing Ad** — clear |
| An agent profile ad | Agent Ad | **Agent Ad**, Agent Spotlight, Agent Card | **Agent Spotlight** — warmer, more premium |
| A brand/custom ad | Brand Ad | **Brand Ad**, Custom Ad, Promo | **Brand Ad** — clear |
| A multi-listing carousel | Collection Ad | **Showcase**, Gallery, Collection, Carousel | **Showcase** — "Create a showcase of your best listings." More evocative than "collection." |
| The visual template for an ad | Layout | **Layout**, Template, Style, Format | **Layout** — already used, fine |
| The color scheme | Theme | **Theme**, Style, Palette, Look | **Theme** — already used, fine |

### Content Organization

| Thing | Current Name | Candidates | Recommendation |
|-------|-------------|------------|----------------|
| An ordered set of ads that loops | Playlist | **Playlist**, Reel, Loop, Rotation, Channel | **Playlist** — everyone knows what a playlist is. Don't overthink it. |
| The full-screen ad display | Slideshow | **Slideshow**, Loop, Rotation, Broadcast | **Slideshow** — clear and accurate |
| A physical location | Site | **Site**, Location, Office, Property, Venue | **Location** — more natural in conversation. "Add a location" vs "Add a site." |

**Alternative for Playlist:** **Reel** has energy — "Create a reel
of your top listings" — and plays on the media/content creation
language agents already know from Instagram. But it might imply video.
Stick with Playlist unless you want to differentiate from competitors.

### Hardware & Setup

| Thing | Current Name | Candidates | Recommendation |
|-------|-------------|------------|----------------|
| A logical display target | Screen | **Screen**, Display, Monitor | **Screen** — matches the physical thing |
| The 6-character code | Pairing Code | **Pairing Code**, Sync Code, Setup Code, Quick Code | **Pairing Code** — self-explanatory |
| The pairing process | Pairing | **Pairing**, Connecting, Linking, Syncing | **Pairing** — Bluetooth established this UX pattern |
| Online/connected status | Online | **Live**, Online, Connected, Active | **Live** — "Your screen is live" is more exciting than "online" |
| Offline/disconnected | Offline | **Offline**, Disconnected, Sleeping | **Offline** — clear |
| No content assigned | Idle | **Idle**, Standby, Waiting, Empty | **Standby** — "Your screen is on standby" sounds intentional, not broken |

### Lead Capture & Engagement

| Thing | Current Name | Candidates | Recommendation |
|-------|-------------|------------|----------------|
| A QR code on an ad | QR Code | **QR Code**, Scan Code, Quick Link, Tap Code | **QR Code** — don't rename what everyone already knows |
| Someone scanning the QR | QR Scan | **Scan**, Tap, Visit, Engagement | **Scan** — simple |
| A captured prospect | Lead | **Lead**, Inquiry, Connection, Contact, Prospect | **Lead** — real estate industry standard |
| The lead list | Lead Inbox | **Inbox**, Pipeline, Lead Board, Lead Feed | **Inbox** — email-like metaphor agents understand |
| The public listing page | Go Page | **Listing Page**, Property Page, Detail Page | No public name needed — it's just the page visitors land on after scanning |

### Analytics

| Thing | Current Name | Candidates | Recommendation |
|-------|-------------|------------|----------------|
| An ad being shown | Impression | **View**, Impression, Display, Play | **View** — simpler for agents. "Your ad got 1,200 views this week." |
| A QR code being scanned | Scan | **Scan**, Tap, Engagement, Click | **Scan** — matches the action |
| A lead being captured | Conversion | **Lead**, Capture, Conversion, Sign-up | **Lead** — don't introduce "conversion" to agents who think in leads |
| The analytics dashboard | Analytics | **Insights**, Analytics, Performance, Stats, Report | **Insights** — "Check your insights" feels more valuable than "check your analytics" |

## Naming Decisions Still Open

1. **"Showcase" vs "Collection"** — "Showcase" is warmer and more
   marketable, but "Collection" is what's in the codebase. Could
   keep "Collection" as the technical term and "Showcase" as the
   UI-facing label. Or rename the model.

2. **"Location" vs "Site"** — "Location" is more natural in
   conversation. "Site" is what's in the codebase. Low-stakes —
   could just relabel in the UI without renaming the model.

3. **"Agent Spotlight" vs "Agent Ad"** — "Spotlight" is warmer. But
   if agents create their own, "Create your spotlight" might sound
   odd. "Create your agent ad" is clearer. Could use "Spotlight" in
   marketing and "Agent Ad" in the builder.

4. **"Insights" vs "Analytics"** — "Insights" is friendlier.
   "Analytics" is more precise. Real estate agents probably prefer
   "Insights." Developers prefer "Analytics." Use "Insights" in the
   UI, "analytics" in technical docs.

5. **"RePlay Go" as an umbrella** — Do you need a portable product
   line name? Only matters if you're selling Kiosks and Tablets as
   distinct SKUs. If it's just one portable device, "RePlay Kiosk"
   is enough.

6. **"View" vs "Impression"** — "View" is simpler for agents but
   potentially ambiguous (page view? ad view?). "Impression" is
   industry standard but jargon-y. Could use "ad view" to
   disambiguate, or just "view" and let context do the work.

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- **Don't brand everything.** "RePlay SmartSync Pairing" is worse
  than "Pairing Code." Save the brand prefix for product lines.
- **Don't use internal model names in the UI.** `ScreenPlaylist` is
  a join table. The user sees "assign a playlist to a screen," not
  "create a screen playlist."
- **Don't rename things agents already know.** A lead is a lead. A
  QR code is a QR code. A listing is a listing. Inventing new terms
  for known concepts creates friction.
- **Don't use "smart" or "AI" unless there's actual intelligence.**
  "Smart Display" implies the display is doing something smart. If
  it's just looping ads, it's not smart — it's a playlist.
- **Don't suffix everything with "Pro" or "Plus."** That's a pricing
  tier decision, not a naming decision. If you need tiers later, the
  product names stay the same — you add Starter/Pro/Enterprise.

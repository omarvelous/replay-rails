# Landing Page Analysis: Digital Signage for Real Estate Brokerages

## Objective

Build a high-converting landing page targeting real estate brokerages
who need digital window displays and lobby screens. Replace the current
placeholder marketing home page.

## Target Audience: Real Estate Brokerages

### Who decides

- **Broker/Owner** — budget authority, cares about ROI and brand
- **Office Manager** — day-to-day operations, cares about ease of use
- **Marketing Director** — (larger firms) cares about brand consistency

### Pain points (in their language)

1. **"Printing new cards every time a listing sells or a price changes"**
   The manual update cycle is the #1 frustration. Staff time + printing
   costs + stale displays.

2. **"Our window display shows sold properties for weeks"**
   Embarrassing, unprofessional, drives zero walk-in traffic.

3. **"We can't show video walkthroughs or virtual tours in the window"**
   Static cards show one photo and basic details; they can't convey
   property appeal.

4. **"Managing signs across multiple offices is chaos"**
   No centralized control, inconsistent branding, no inventory tracking.

5. **"I don't need another complex platform to learn"**
   45% of brokers say technology is their greatest challenge. They want
   simple, not powerful.

### Language to use (speak real estate, not SaaS)

| Say this | Not this |
|----------|----------|
| Window displays | Digital signage |
| Lobby screens | Endpoints |
| Walk-in traffic | Impressions |
| Listing marketing | Content management |
| Storefront | Deployment |
| Your listings | Your content |

## Conversion Benchmarks

### B2B SaaS landing page benchmarks (2026)

- Average conversion: 2-5%
- Top performers: 8-15%
- Single CTA pages: 13.5% (vs 10.5% for multi-CTA — 28% lift)
- Product screenshot in hero outperforms illustrations
- 57% of page-viewing time is above the fold

### Free trial vs demo (by ACV)

| Model | Conversion rate | Best for |
|-------|----------------|----------|
| Free trial | 18% | ACV under $15K, self-serve |
| Demo | 32% | ACV above $15K, multi-office |
| Freemium | 2-5% | Only at massive scale |

### Recommendation for RePlay

Primary CTA: **"Start free trial"** — single office brokerages are
sub-$15K ACV, self-serve trial is the right motion.

Secondary CTA: **"Book a demo"** as text link — captures multi-office
brokerages with higher ACV.

## Recommended Page Structure

### 1. Hero (above the fold)

- **Headline:** Outcome-focused, not feature-focused
  - Option A: "Your window listings, always up to date"
  - Option B: "Turn your storefront into your best-performing agent"
  - Option C: "Never show a sold listing again"
- **Subhead:** One sentence connecting to MLS sync
  - "Listings sync automatically. Sold properties disappear. New
    listings appear. No printing, no staff time."
- **CTA:** "Start free trial" (primary button, contrasting color)
  - "Book a demo" as secondary text link
- **Visual:** Real product screenshot — storefront window with a
  RePlay-powered display showing listings. Not a mockup, not an
  illustration. The product itself.
- **No navigation links** — minimize exit points

### 2. Logo bar / social proof strip

- 6-10 logos (brokerage brands, MLS systems, or integration partners)
- Or metric strip: "X listings · Y screens · Z brokerages"
- G2/Capterra badge if available

### 3. Pain point section (problem → solution)

Three columns, each structured as:
- Icon or illustration
- Pain headline: "Stale window displays lose walk-ins"
- One-line solution: "Listings sync from your MLS in real time"

Suggested three:
1. **Stale listings** → Auto-sync from MLS
2. **Manual updates** → Set it and forget it
3. **Static cards** → Video tours, QR codes, dynamic content

### 4. Feature walkthrough

3-4 features with product screenshots:
1. **MLS auto-sync** — "Add a listing, it appears on screen"
2. **Ad builder** — "Turn any listing into signage in one click"
3. **QR lead capture** — "Walk-by scans turn into leads in your inbox"
4. **Multi-office control** — "Manage every screen from one dashboard"

### 5. Integration proof point

- Row of MLS logos (Bright MLS, CRMLS, Stellar, etc.)
- "Works with your MLS" messaging
- Future: CRM integration logos

### 6. Testimonial / case study block

- One video testimonial > ten written quotes
- Target quote: "I used to spend 2 hours a week updating window cards"
- Include name, photo, brokerage name, role

### 7. Pricing transparency

- Show plans with prices — pricing transparency is a trust signal
- Suggested tiers align with roadmap:
  - Starter (1 screen, $49/mo)
  - Professional (5 screens, $149/mo)
  - Enterprise (custom)

### 8. Final CTA repeat

- Same "Start free trial" CTA
- Add urgency or reassurance: "No credit card required" or
  "Set up in under 5 minutes"

## What to Avoid

- **Hiding pricing** — SMB brokerages won't bother with a sales call
- **Generic "digital signage" language** — speak their language
- **Multiple competing CTAs** — single CTA pages convert 28% better
- **Illustrations instead of product** — show the real product
- **Feature dumps** — lead with outcomes, not specs
- **Long forms** — email-only signup, expand later

---

## Differentiator to Own

**MLS auto-sync is the wedge.** Every competitor mentions it; none make
it the hero. The manual update cycle is the universal pain point. RePlay
should be the platform that makes "your listings are always current"
feel inevitable, not aspirational.

The messaging hierarchy:
1. Your listings stay current (outcome)
2. Because they sync from your MLS (mechanism)
3. So you never print another window card (pain removed)
4. And every walk-by can become a lead (bonus value)

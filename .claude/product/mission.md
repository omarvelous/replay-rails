# Product Mission

## Problem

Every brokerage, leasing office, and sales gallery has a street-facing window displaying printed flyers that are outdated within days and invisible at night. A TV + a $30 streaming stick can turn that dead space into a 24/7 lead generation surface — but nobody wants to manage another piece of software.

## Target Users

**Office Manager (primary)** — Runs the day-to-day of a brokerage office. Not technical. Wants the window to look professional and current. Sets it and forgets it, updating only when listings change.

**Marketing Manager** — Oversees multiple offices. Wants brand consistency, scheduling control, and performance data (QR scan analytics).

**Passerby / Prospective Buyer** — Walks past the window, scans a QR code, lands on a mobile property detail page. Never logs in — consumer of the output only.

## Solution

RePlay is digital signage purpose-built for real estate storefronts. It replaces static printed property sheets with managed, dynamic displays and connects passersby to listings via QR codes.

### Why RePlay wins

- **Listing-native** — Create an ad from a listing in two clicks, not by uploading a JPG
- **Agent-aware** — Agent branding, contact info, and compliance fields baked in
- **QR-to-lead pipeline** — Every screen is a lead source, not just a display
- **Priced for a single office** — Not enterprise signage pricing
- **15-minute setup** — A non-technical office manager can go from signup to a live screen in under 15 minutes
